A LETTER FOR PRAGYA

This Diwali · With all my love

My dearest Pragya,

I wanted to give you something that would last longer than a box of sweets or a few beautiful lights. So I made you this little corner of the internet—a place you can return to whenever you want a reminder of how much you mean to me.

Sometimes I think about how unexpectedly our story found its way here. A conversation, a few honest words, the little moments that slowly became important, and then that beautiful day when you said yes. I don't take any of it for granted.

I love the way you bring adventure into life. I think about our walks in Vrindavan, our time in Barsana, and Rishikesh—especially seeing you take on bungee jumping. I love that there is always something new to discover with you. Even the simplest time together becomes a memory I want to keep.

I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise to keep showing up, to keep learning how to love you better, to respect your dreams, to laugh with you, and to stand beside you when life gets difficult. I want us to build a life where both of us feel safe to be ourselves and excited about what comes next.

Thank you for being you, Pragya. Thank you for your love, your trust, your warmth, and for making ordinary days feel special. If I could choose one thing to carry into every tomorrow, it would be the chance to keep making you smile.

You are not just a part of my happiness.
You are one of the reasons I believe in it.

Forever choosing you,
Prasoon ♡
